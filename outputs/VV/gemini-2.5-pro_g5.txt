Varicose veins are veins in your leg that look swollen, twisted, and blue. They are close to the skin. High pressure in your leg veins is a cause, and they can also run in families.

Many people have varicose veins. If you do not treat them, they can get worse over time. They can make your legs ache or feel tired. You might also get rashes, redness, or open sores on your skin.

Varicose veins happen because it is hard for veins to move blood from your feet back up to your heart. Treatment can help you feel better. It also helps the blood flow in your legs and your whole body.

You may have varicose veins if you see swollen or twisted veins on your legs. You might also see thin, web-like veins called spider veins.

Your legs might feel heavy or achy, especially after you stand or sit for a long time. You could have swelling in your ankles. Some people feel itching or burning around the veins.

Other signs can be skin that looks red or sore. You might also get leg cramps or feel like you need to move your legs, especially at night. These feelings can get worse if the veins are not treated.

Certain things can make you more likely to get varicose veins. Getting older is one reason, especially for people between 30 and 70 years old.

Being pregnant can cause varicose veins, but they often go away after the baby is born. Having more than one baby can sometimes cause varicose veins that do not go away.

Other things that increase your chances are having family members with varicose veins or being overweight. Standing or sitting for long periods of time can also be a cause.

To find out if you have varicose veins, you will see a special doctor who treats blood vessel problems. The doctor will ask about your health and look at the veins in your legs.

The doctor may send you for a special ultrasound test. This test does not hurt. It uses sound waves to look at your veins and see how the blood is flowing inside them. This helps the doctor understand the problem.

Your doctor will likely suggest some changes you can make at home first. Keeping a healthy weight is important.

You should also try to put your feet up when you are resting. Try not to sit or stand for too long without a break. Your doctor might also tell you to wear special tight socks or stockings to help with blood flow.

If these changes are not enough, your doctor may suggest a treatment to fix the veins. One treatment is called sclerotherapy. This is mostly for smaller spider veins. The doctor injects a medicine into the vein that makes it close up.

For larger varicose veins, a doctor might do a procedure called vein stripping. The doctor makes two small cuts and removes the problem vein. You can usually go home the same day and get back to your normal life in about a month.

Other treatments use heat from a laser or radio waves to close the vein. A doctor puts a tiny tube into the vein to deliver the heat. This is often used instead of vein stripping. Sometimes, the doctor will also remove clumps of veins through tiny cuts at the same time.

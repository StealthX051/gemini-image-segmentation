Varicose veins are swollen, twisted veins you can see on your legs. They often look blue and stick out from the skin. High pressure in your leg veins causes them. It can also run in your family.

Many people have varicose veins. If you do not treat them, they can get worse over time. They can make your legs ache or feel tired. They can also cause skin problems like rashes or sores.

Your veins work hard to move blood from your feet back up to your heart. Sometimes, this pressure can cause varicose veins. Treatment helps you feel better and improves how blood moves in your body.

You might see swollen or twisted veins on your legs. Some smaller veins look like a spider's web.

Your legs may feel heavy or achy, especially after you stand or sit for a long time. You might have swelling in your ankles, or your legs might itch or burn. Some people get leg cramps at night.

Certain things can make you more likely to get varicose veins. Getting older is one reason.

Being pregnant can cause them, but they often go away after the baby is born. Having more than one baby can sometimes cause veins that do not go away.

If others in your family have them, you might get them too. Being overweight or standing for long periods of time can also be a cause.

A doctor who treats blood vessels will ask about your health and look at your legs. This helps the doctor understand your problem.

Your doctor may send you for a special sound wave test. This test does not hurt. It uses sound to take pictures of your veins and see how your blood is flowing inside them.

Your doctor may first suggest simple changes to help your legs feel better. Keeping a healthy weight is important.

You can also put your feet up when you are resting. Try not to sit or stand for too long without a break. Your doctor might also tell you to wear special tight socks to help with blood flow.

If simple changes are not enough, your doctor may suggest a treatment to fix the veins. One treatment is called sclerotherapy. The doctor injects a medicine into the vein to close it off. This is often used for smaller spider veins.

Another treatment is called vein stripping. A doctor makes small cuts to remove the problem veins. You can usually go home the same day. It may take a few weeks to get back to your normal activities.

Newer treatments use heat from a laser or radio waves. A doctor puts a tiny tube into the vein. The heat from the tube closes the vein. These treatments are often used instead of vein stripping.

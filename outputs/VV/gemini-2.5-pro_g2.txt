Varicose veins are veins in your leg that look swollen and twisted. They are close to the skin and can look blue.

High pressure in your leg veins causes them. It can also be something that runs in your family. Many people have them.

If you do not get them treated, they can get worse. They can make your legs hurt or feel tired. They can also cause rashes or sores on your skin.

Veins move blood from your feet back to your heart. Gravity makes this hard work for your veins. This can cause varicose veins.

Treatment can help you feel better. It also helps the blood flow in your legs and body.

You may see veins that are swollen and twisted. You might also see tiny veins that look like a spider web.

Your legs may feel heavy or hurt, especially after standing for a long time. Your ankles may get swollen.

The skin around the veins can feel itchy. Your skin might get red or have sores. You may also get leg cramps at night.

Getting older can make you more likely to have these veins. Being pregnant can also cause them. They usually go away after the baby is born.

If you have many babies, the veins might not go away. You might get them if others in your family have them.

Being overweight is a risk. Standing or sitting for a long time can also cause them.

A special doctor for blood vessels will check your legs. The doctor will ask about your health and look at your veins.

You may need a special test. The test uses sound to see inside your veins. It does not hurt.

This test helps the doctor see your veins and how your blood is flowing. This helps find the cause of the problem.

Your doctor may suggest changes to help your legs. Staying at a healthy weight can help.

Putting your legs up when you rest is good. You should try not to sit or stand for too long.

Your doctor may ask you to wear special tight socks. These socks help with blood flow in your legs.

Sometimes, the doctor may suggest other treatments. For small spider veins, a doctor can give you a shot.

The shot has medicine that closes the vein. This stops blood from going into it. This is done in the doctor's office.

For bigger veins, a doctor may do a surgery to take out the bad vein. The doctor makes two small cuts to remove it.

You will get medicine to make you sleep during this. You can go home the same day. It takes about four weeks to get back to your normal life.

Another treatment uses heat to close the bad vein. A doctor puts a tiny tube into the vein.

The tube sends out heat that closes the vein. You will get medicine so you do not feel it. This can be done with another treatment that takes out small veins through tiny cuts.

Varicose veins are veins in your legs that look swollen and twisted. They are often blue and close to your skin. They happen when there is too much pressure in the veins. If people in your family have them, you might get them too.

Many people have varicose veins. If they are not treated, they can get worse. They can make your legs ache or feel tired. Sometimes they can cause rashes or sores on your skin.

Treatment can help you feel better. It also helps keep your legs and blood flow healthy.

You may see veins that are swollen or stick out. You might also see small, thin veins that look like a spider's web.

Your legs might ache or feel heavy. This can happen after you stand or sit for a long time. Your ankles may swell, and the skin around the veins might itch or burn.

Your legs might hurt, or you could get cramps at night. These problems can get worse if you do not see a doctor.

Some things make you more likely to get varicose veins. Getting older is one reason. You are also more likely to get them if people in your family have them.

Many women get them when they are pregnant. They often go away after the baby is born. Being overweight or standing for a long time can also cause them.

A special doctor can check your veins. The doctor will ask you questions and look at your legs.

The doctor may send you for a test called an ultrasound. This test does not hurt. It uses sound waves to take a picture of your veins. This helps the doctor see how blood is moving in your legs.

Your doctor may suggest simple changes to help your legs. Keeping a healthy weight is important. You can also put your feet up when you rest.

Try not to sit or stand for too long without moving. Your doctor might tell you to wear special tight socks. These are called compression stockings.

If simple changes do not work, your doctor may suggest a treatment to fix the veins. One treatment is a shot of medicine into the vein. This is called sclerotherapy. It closes the vein so blood cannot flow through it.

Another treatment is called vein stripping. The doctor makes small cuts in your skin and removes the bad vein. You will be asleep for this and can go home the same day.

Doctors can also use heat to close a bad vein. They put a tiny tube into the vein. The tube uses heat from a laser or radio waves to seal the vein shut. This treatment is often used instead of vein stripping.

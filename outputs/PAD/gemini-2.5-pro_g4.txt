Peripheral Artery Disease, or PAD, is a problem with the blood vessels that carry blood to your arms and legs. These blood vessels can become narrow or blocked. This makes it hard for blood to flow.

You may have heard of a heart attack, which is a blockage in the blood vessels of the heart. A similar blockage can happen in your arms and legs. When this happens, your arms and legs do not get enough oxygen and food from your blood.

If PAD is not treated, it can become very serious. It may lead to the loss of a leg or arm.

Healthy blood vessels are open so blood can flow easily. As you get older, a sticky stuff called plaque can build up inside your blood vessels. Plaque is made of fat and cholesterol. This buildup makes the blood vessels narrow and hard, which makes it difficult for blood to pass through.

A common sign of PAD is pain in your legs or arms when you are active, like when you walk. The pain might be in your calves, thighs, or bottom. This pain usually goes away when you stop and rest.

If PAD gets worse, you might feel pain in your legs or arms even when you are resting. This pain can be worse at night. You may also get sores on your feet that do not heal.

Some things make you more likely to get PAD. These include being older, being a man, and smoking. Having high blood pressure, diabetes, or high cholesterol also raises your risk. Being very overweight, not exercising, or having family members with blood vessel problems can also be a cause.

Not all leg pain is from PAD. But if you are older and have leg pain, you should talk to your doctor. This is very important if you have any of the risks for PAD.

If you have pain when you are resting or have foot sores that won't heal, you should see a doctor right away.

A doctor can find out if you have PAD with simple tests that do not hurt. These tests check the blood pressure and blood flow in your legs.

If you have signs of PAD, your doctor may send you to a vascular surgeon. This is a special doctor who treats problems with blood vessels. They can help you with all types of treatment.

Treatment for PAD can include making healthy life changes. Quitting smoking and exercising every day can help a lot. Sometimes, medicine is also needed.

If the problem is more serious, a procedure or surgery may be needed to help blood flow better. It is important to treat PAD to keep your arms and legs healthy.

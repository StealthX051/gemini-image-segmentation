Peripheral Artery Disease, or PAD, is a problem with the blood vessels in your legs and arms. These vessels get narrow or blocked. This makes it hard for blood to get to your limbs.

You may have heard of blocked blood vessels in the heart. The same thing can happen in your arms and legs. This is called PAD. Without enough blood, your arms and legs do not get the food and oxygen they need.

Healthy blood vessels are open so blood can flow easily. As you get older, a sticky stuff called plaque can build up inside them. Plaque is made of fat. It makes the blood vessels narrow and hard.

A common sign of PAD is pain in your legs or arms when you walk or move around. The pain goes away when you stop and rest. This happens because your muscles are not getting enough blood when you are active.

If PAD gets worse, you may feel pain even when you are resting, like at night. You might also get sores on your feet that do not heal. These are serious signs.

Some things make you more likely to get PAD. These include being older and being a man. Having high blood pressure, diabetes, or high cholesterol also raises your risk.

Smoking, being overweight, and not exercising can also lead to PAD. If people in your family have had blood vessel problems, you may be at higher risk too.

Not all leg pain is from PAD. But if you are older and have leg pain when you move, talk to your doctor. Ask if you should be checked for PAD.

It is very important to see a doctor right away if you have pain when you are resting. You should also see a doctor for foot sores that will not heal.

Your doctor can do a simple test to check for PAD. The test uses sound waves and checks the blood pressure in your legs. It does not hurt.

If you have signs of PAD, your doctor may send you to a special doctor called a vascular surgeon. This doctor knows all the ways to treat PAD. They can give you medicine or do a procedure to help.

Treatment for PAD often starts with healthy changes. This includes quitting smoking and exercising every day. Your doctor may also give you medicine.

It is important to treat PAD. If it gets worse, it can cause serious problems. Treatment helps keep your legs healthy and can save your life.

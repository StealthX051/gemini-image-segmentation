PAD is a problem with the tubes that carry blood to your arms and legs. These tubes can get narrow or blocked.

You may know that a block in the tubes to your heart is a heart attack. A block can also happen in your arms and legs. This stops your arms and legs from getting the food and air they need from your blood. If not treated, this can be very serious.

Healthy blood tubes are open so blood can flow easily. As you get older, a sticky stuff called plaque can build up inside. This plaque is made of fat. It makes the tubes narrow and hard.

A sign of PAD is pain in your legs or arms when you move around. The pain goes away when you stop and rest. This happens because your muscles are not getting enough blood when you are active.

If PAD gets worse, you may feel pain even when you are not moving. This pain can be worse at night. You might also get sores on your feet that do not heal.

Some things make you more likely to get PAD. Getting older is one reason. Men are more likely to get it than women.

Having high blood pressure, diabetes, or high cholesterol also puts you at risk. Smoking, being overweight, and not exercising can cause PAD. If your family has had blood vessel problems, you might be at risk too.

Pain in your legs is not always PAD. But if you are older and have leg pain when you walk, you should talk to your doctor. Ask your doctor if you should be checked for PAD.

It is very important to see a doctor right away if you have pain when you rest. You should also see a doctor if you have foot sores that will not heal.

A doctor can find out if you have PAD with simple tests. These tests do not hurt. They check the blood pressure in your legs and use sound waves to look at your blood flow.

Your doctor may send you to a special doctor called a vascular surgeon. This doctor knows a lot about blood vessel problems. They can give you medicine or do a procedure to help.

There are ways to treat PAD. Your doctor may ask you to make healthy changes. This includes quitting smoking and exercising every day. You may also need to take medicine.

If PAD is very bad, it needs to be treated. Treatment can save your leg or arm. It can also save your life.

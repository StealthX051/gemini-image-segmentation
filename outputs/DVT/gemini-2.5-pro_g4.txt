Deep vein thrombosis, or DVT, is a serious health problem. It happens when a blood clot forms in a deep vein, which is a large blood vessel. This usually happens in the leg.

The blood clot can block blood from flowing like it should. If the clot breaks free, it can travel to your lungs. This is very dangerous and is called a pulmonary embolism.

DVT is an emergency. You need to see a doctor right away to get help and prevent serious problems.

You may have DVT if you notice certain signs in one of your legs. Your leg might be swollen, often in the lower leg or thigh. It may feel painful or sore, especially when you stand or walk.

The skin on your leg might feel warm or look red. You might also see a blue or red color on the skin. Some people feel like their leg is heavy or tired.

If a clot travels to your lungs, you might suddenly feel short of breath or have chest pain. You might also have a fast heartbeat or cough up blood. These are signs of an emergency, and you should get medical help right away.

You should talk to a doctor if you have any signs of DVT. These signs include swelling, pain, warmth, or redness in one leg. It is very important to get checked early to prevent other problems.

Be sure to tell your doctor if you have things that make you more likely to get DVT. These are called risk factors.

Many things can make you more likely to get a DVT. Not moving for a long time, like on a long trip or after surgery, is a common cause. Having surgery, especially on your legs or belly, can also raise your risk.

An injury to your leg, like a broken bone, can lead to a clot. Being overweight, being pregnant, or taking birth control pills can also increase your risk. Smoking is another thing that can harm your blood vessels.

Your risk for DVT also goes up as you get older, especially after age 60. Some health problems like cancer or heart disease can make clots more likely. If family members have had blood clots, your risk might be higher too.

A doctor will do a few things to find out if you have DVT. They will ask you about your health and how you feel. They will also check your leg for swelling, pain, or warmth.

You may have a blood test called a D-dimer test. This test looks for a substance that is released when a blood clot breaks down. It helps the doctor see if a clot might be there.

The doctor will likely use a special test to look at your veins. The most common test is a Doppler ultrasound. This test uses sound waves to create a picture of the blood flowing in your veins and can show if there is a clot.

Sometimes, other tests are needed to get a better look. These tests use X-rays or other machines to take detailed pictures of your veins. Finding a DVT early is the best way to get the right treatment.

Your doctor will choose the best treatment for you. Most people with DVT are treated with medicines called blood thinners. These medicines help stop the clot from getting bigger and prevent new clots from forming.

Your doctor may also tell you to wear special tight stockings called compression stockings. These help with swelling and pain in your leg. Keeping your leg raised up can also help reduce swelling.

In serious cases, a doctor might use strong medicine to break up the clot quickly. Another option is a small filter placed in a large vein to catch clots before they reach the lungs.

Sometimes, a procedure may be needed to remove a very large blood clot. Your doctor will talk with you about the best plan. It is important to follow your doctor's instructions to stay healthy.

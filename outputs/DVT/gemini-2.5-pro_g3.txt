A deep vein thrombosis, or DVT, is a blood clot in a vein deep inside your body. These clots usually happen in the leg. A blood clot can block blood from moving like it should.

This is a serious problem that needs a doctor's help right away. Sometimes, a piece of the clot can break off and go to the lungs. This is very dangerous and can make it hard to breathe.

You may have a blood clot if one of your legs is swollen. The leg might feel sore or tender, especially when you stand or walk.

The skin on your leg may feel warm or look red or blue. Your leg might also feel heavy or tired.

If you suddenly feel short of breath or have chest pain, get help right away. This could mean the clot has moved to your lungs.

It is very important to call a doctor if you have any signs of a blood clot. Tell your doctor if you have things that make you more likely to get a clot. Getting help quickly can stop serious problems from happening.

Some things can make you more likely to get a blood clot. Not moving for a long time, like on a long trip or after surgery, is a risk. Having surgery or a bad injury can also cause a clot.

Being overweight, being pregnant, or smoking can raise your risk. Some medicines, like birth control pills, can also be a risk. Your risk is higher if you are older than 60 or if family members have had clots.

A special doctor for blood vessels will help find out if you have a clot. The doctor will ask you questions and look at your leg. You may need a blood test to check for clots.

The most common test is an ultrasound. This test uses sound waves to make a picture of the blood flow in your veins. It does not hurt.

Your doctor will choose the best treatment for you. Most people take medicine called blood thinners. These medicines help stop the clot from getting bigger and prevent new clots.

Your doctor might tell you to wear special tight stockings. These stockings help with swelling and pain by improving blood flow. Keeping your leg raised up can also help reduce swelling.

In serious cases, a doctor may use strong medicine to break up the clot quickly. Sometimes, a small filter is put in a large vein to catch clots before they reach the lungs. In rare cases, a doctor may need to do a procedure to remove the clot.

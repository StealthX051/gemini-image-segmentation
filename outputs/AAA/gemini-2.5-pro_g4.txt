The aorta is the biggest blood vessel in your body. It carries blood from your heart down through your belly. It sends blood to your legs, stomach, and kidneys.

An abdominal aortic aneurysm, or AAA, is a weak spot in the wall of this blood vessel in your belly. This weak spot can swell up like a small balloon. If the bulge gets too big, it can break open. This is very dangerous and causes bleeding inside your body.

Sometimes, small blood clots can form in the bulge. These clots can travel and block blood flow to other parts of your body, like your legs. This can cause a lot of pain.

Most people with this problem do not feel any symptoms. It is often found by accident during a test for something else. If you do have symptoms, you might feel pain in your belly or groin. You might also feel a throbbing, like a heartbeat, in your belly.

An AAA happens when the wall of the aorta gets weak. Certain things can make you more likely to get one. These are called risk factors.

Being over 60 years old and being a man are risk factors. Smoking, having high blood pressure, or having a family member with an AAA also raises your risk. Hardening of the arteries (called atherosclerosis) is another risk factor.

If you have Medicare, you may be able to get a free one-time test for an AAA. This test is for certain people when they first join Medicare. Ask your doctor if you can get this test.

If you have been told you have an AAA, you should see a special doctor. This doctor is called a vascular surgeon. Your main doctor can help you see this specialist.

To check for an AAA, your doctor may use a test called an ultrasound. This test uses sound waves to make a picture of the blood vessel in your belly. It is a safe and painless test. Another test is a CTA scan, which uses X-rays and a special dye to get a detailed picture.

A vascular surgeon is an expert in blood vessel problems. They will decide if your AAA needs to be treated now or if it is safe to watch it. They will help choose the best treatment for you.

If the bulge is small, your doctor may just watch it closely. You might need to make changes like quitting smoking or taking medicine for your blood pressure. You will have regular checkups to see if the bulge is growing.

If the bulge is large or growing, it may need to be fixed. One way to fix it is with a small tube, called a stent graft, that is placed inside the blood vessel. Another way is with open surgery to repair the weak spot.

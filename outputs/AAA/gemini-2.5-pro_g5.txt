The aorta is the largest blood vessel in your body. It carries blood from your heart down through your chest and into your belly. From your belly, it sends blood to your legs, stomach, and kidneys.

An abdominal aortic aneurysm, or AAA, is a weak spot in the wall of the aorta in your belly. This weak spot can swell or bulge like a balloon.

If the bulge gets too big, it can burst. A burst aneurysm causes dangerous bleeding inside your body and can be deadly. Sometimes, blood clots can form in the bulge. These clots can travel and block blood flow to other parts of your body, like your legs.

Most people with an AAA do not feel any symptoms. The problem is often found by accident during a test for something else. If you do have symptoms, you might feel pain in your belly or groin. You might also notice a throbbing feeling in your belly.

An AAA happens when the wall of the aorta gets weak. Certain things can make you more likely to get one. These are called risk factors.

Being over 60 years old is a risk factor. Men are more likely to get an AAA than women. Other risks include smoking, having high blood pressure, or having a family member who had an AAA. Hardening of the arteries (a condition called atherosclerosis) is also a risk.

If you have Medicare, you may be able to get a free one-time screening test for an AAA. This test is an ultrasound. Ask your doctor if you can get this free screening. If you have risk factors for an AAA, you should talk to your doctor.

If your doctor thinks you might have an AAA, they will order a test. The most common test is an ultrasound of your belly. This test uses sound waves to take a picture and does not hurt.

Your doctor might also order a special X-ray called a CTA scan. For this test, you will get a special dye in your vein to help the doctor see your blood vessels clearly. These tests show how big the aneurysm is and where it is.

You may need to see a vascular surgeon. This is a doctor who is an expert in treating blood vessel problems. The surgeon will decide if you need treatment and what kind of treatment is best for you.

Treatment depends on the size of the aneurysm. If it is small, your doctor will watch it closely with regular checkups. You may need to make healthy changes like quitting smoking or taking medicine to lower your blood pressure.

If the aneurysm is large or growing, you may need a procedure to fix it. One option is a procedure where a doctor places a small tube inside the blood vessel to make it stronger. Another option is open surgery to replace the weak part of the blood vessel. Your doctor will talk with you about the best choice for your health.

The aorta is the biggest blood vessel in your body. It carries blood from your heart down into your belly. It sends blood to your legs, stomach, and kidneys.

An Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm is a weak spot in this blood vessel in your belly. We will call it an AAA. The wall of the blood vessel gets weak and starts to swell like a balloon.

If an AAA is not treated, it can grow and burst. This causes bleeding inside your body and can be very unsafe.

Small blood clots can also form in an AAA. These clots can move and block blood flow in other parts of your body. This can cause a lot of pain or even lead to losing a leg if blood flow is stopped for too long.

Most people with an AAA have no signs. A doctor often finds it by chance during a test for something else. If there are signs, you may feel pain in your belly or back.

An AAA is caused when the wall of the aorta gets weak. Some things make this more likely to happen. People over 60 years old and men are more at risk.

Your risk is also higher if you smoke or have high blood pressure. A family member with an AAA or hard blood vessels also raises your risk.

Medicare offers a free, one-time test for an AAA for some older adults. Ask your doctor if you can get this test. It is for men who have smoked or for people with a family history of AAA.

If your doctor thinks you might have an AAA, you will get a test. A common test is an ultrasound of your belly. This test uses sound waves to see inside you and does not hurt.

You may also have a special x-ray test. This test helps the doctor see the size and place of the AAA.

You may see a special blood vessel doctor called a vascular surgeon. This doctor will decide if you need treatment. They will help you choose the best plan for you.

If the AAA is small, your doctor will check it often. You may need to quit smoking or take medicine to lower your blood pressure.

If the AAA is large or growing, it may need to be fixed with surgery. One way is to place a small tube inside the blood vessel to make it strong. Another way is a bigger surgery to fix the weak spot.

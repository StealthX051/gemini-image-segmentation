The aorta is the main blood vessel in your body. It carries blood from your heart down into your belly area. It sends blood to your legs and other parts of your body.

An abdominal aortic aneurysm is a weak spot in the aorta in your belly. The wall of the blood vessel gets weak and starts to bulge out. This bulge is called an aneurysm.

If the bulge gets too big, it can break open. This is called a rupture. A rupture causes very bad bleeding inside your body and is very dangerous.

Blood clots can also form inside the bulge. These clots can travel and block blood flow in other places, like your legs. This can cause a lot of pain.

Most people with this problem do not feel any symptoms. Doctors often find it by chance during a test for something else. If you do have signs, you might feel pain in your belly or a pulse in your belly.

This problem happens when the wall of the aorta gets weak. You are more likely to get it if you are older than 60. Men get it more often than women.

You are also at higher risk if you smoke or have high blood pressure. Having hard arteries, also called atherosclerosis, or a lung disease for a long time also raises your risk. If someone in your family had this problem, you might be at risk too.

Medicare may pay for a one-time test to check for this problem. This free test is for some older adults. Men who have smoked and people with a family history of the problem may be able to get it.

If you have risks for this problem, you should talk to your doctor. Your doctor may send you to a special doctor who treats blood vessels. This doctor is called a vascular surgeon.

To see if you have a bulge, your doctor may use an ultrasound test. This test uses sound waves to make a picture of your aorta. The test does not hurt and can show how big the bulge is.

Your doctor might also use a different test called a CTA scan. This test uses X-rays and a special dye to get a very clear picture. It helps the doctor see the bulge in more detail.

A vascular surgeon will decide if you need treatment. They will look at the size of the bulge and your health to choose the best plan for you. They know the different ways to fix the problem.

If the bulge is small, your doctor will watch it with regular checkups. You may need to make healthy changes, like quitting smoking. Your doctor may also give you medicine.

If the bulge is large or growing, it may need to be fixed with surgery. Your doctor will talk with you about the best way to fix it to keep you safe.

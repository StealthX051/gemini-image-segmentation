You have big tubes in your neck called carotid arteries. They carry blood from your heart to your brain. Your brain needs this blood to work.

Sometimes, sticky stuff called plaque builds up in these tubes. This plaque is made of fat. It can make the tubes hard and narrow, which is a problem called carotid artery disease.

This problem can block blood from getting to your brain. This can cause a stroke. A stroke is very serious and needs help right away.

You may not feel sick at first. A stroke can be the first sign. Sometimes you get a warning called a mini-stroke.

A mini-stroke is a serious warning that you need to see a doctor. Its signs can be weakness on one side of your body. Your face may droop on one side.

You may also have trouble seeing or talking. You might have a hard time understanding what people say. If you have any of these signs, get help fast.

Remember the word FAST to check for a stroke. F is for Face drooping. A is for Arm weakness. S is for Speech trouble. T is for Time to call 911.

Things that can cause this problem are getting older or being very overweight. High blood pressure, diabetes, and smoking also raise your risk.

Your doctor may do a test to look at the tubes in your neck. This test is called an ultrasound and it does not hurt. It shows if the tubes are narrow.

You may see a special doctor who treats blood tubes. This doctor will help you find the best way to get better.

Treatment depends on how narrow your tubes are. You may need to change how you eat, exercise more, or take medicine.

Some people need a surgery to clean out the sticky plaque. Another way is to put a small tube inside to hold it open. This can help stop a stroke from happening.

Your neck has two large tubes called carotid arteries. They carry blood from your heart to your brain. When these tubes are healthy, blood flows easily to your brain.

As you get older, a sticky stuff called plaque can build up in these tubes. Plaque is made of fat. It can make your arteries narrow and hard. This is called carotid artery disease. This problem can cause a stroke.

You may not feel sick at first. A stroke could be the first sign that you have a problem. But there are often warning signs called mini-strokes. A mini-stroke is very serious, and you need to get help right away.

Signs of a stroke or mini-stroke can happen suddenly. One side of your body may feel weak or numb. You may not be able to move an arm or a leg.

You may have trouble seeing out of one or both eyes. It might be hard to talk clearly. You may also have trouble understanding what people say.

If you think someone is having a stroke, think F.A.S.T. F is for Face Drooping. A is for Arm Weakness. S is for Speech Difficulty. T is for Time to call 911. If you see any of these signs, call 911 right away.

Some things make you more likely to get this disease. These include getting older, being very overweight, or having high blood pressure. Smoking, not exercising, and having high cholesterol also raise your risk.

If your doctor thinks you have this disease, you will get a test called an ultrasound. This test does not hurt. It uses sound waves to take pictures of the arteries in your neck. The pictures show if your arteries are narrow.

If you have this disease, you may see a special doctor called a vascular surgeon. This doctor is an expert in blood vessels. They can help you decide on the best treatment.

Your treatment will depend on how narrow your arteries are. Your doctor may suggest healthy eating and exercise. You might also need to take medicine.

Sometimes, a procedure is needed to open the artery. One surgery cleans the plaque out of the artery. Another procedure uses a small tube to hold the artery open. Some people need surgery even if they do not feel sick.
